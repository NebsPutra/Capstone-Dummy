# Live demo script (about 8 minutes)

The demo runs on the live site, https://komunitasa.vercel.app. Each step lists what to click and a line to say, in English and Bahasa Indonesia. The slide deck (`flyer/komunitas-project-deck.pptx`, `flyer/PITCH DECK CAPSTONE.pptx`) comes before it, and the backup video is there in case the network fails.

## The day before

1. **Two accounts.** Register them yourselves on the live site; these steps are for people, not scripts.
   - **Organizer**: your main account. It should have admin rights so the analytics page works.
   - **Participant**: a second account on a second phone, with a different email address.
   - Make the two accounts friends: send a request from one profile (Community) and accept it on the other.
   - Ask 2–3 teammates to have their own accounts signed in on their phones (for step 6).
2. **One demo activity.** Sign in as the Organizer and create it:
   - public, starting about 30 minutes after the demo begins, so check-in is open;
   - a banner, and capacity 3, so the waiting list can be shown.
   - Write down its link.
3. **Participant joins it.** Sign in as the Participant and join the activity. This makes the "friends going" line appear for anyone who is friends with them.
4. **Allow notifications.** On the Participant phone, go to Settings and turn on phone notifications.
5. **Check the room.**
   - Load the site on the projector laptop.
   - Zoom the browser to 125%.
   - Turn on Do Not Disturb.
   - Have a phone hotspot ready as a backup network.
6. **Backup video.** Keep it downloaded on the laptop desktop.

## If something fails

- **No network:** switch to the hotspot. If that fails too, play the backup video from step 4 onwards.
- **The QR won't scan:** read out the event code shown under the QR on the Present screen; it can be typed into Explore under "Have an event code?".
- **No push notification:** say "it also arrives by email" and move on. Push notifications depend on the phone's own settings.
- **Check-in says "closed":** check-in opens 1 hour before the start and stays open until 1 hour after the end. The demo activity has to start within the hour.

## After the demo

Leave the demo activity up (it's real data). Or cancel it from its edit page so it doesn't appear in Explore later.

## Real numbers (worth doing)

Run one small real activity a week or more before the defence, for example a 5-person run or book chat.

- Use check-in, then ask people to rate it.
- **Admin, then Analytics, then Participation** will then show a real show-up rate and rating.
- Put those figures on the "End results" slide.
